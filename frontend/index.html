About the Platform

Every admission season in Bangladesh, HSC graduates are forced to monitor 20+ university websites simultaneously. Missing a deadline means missing an opportunity — just because of scattered, inconsistent information.

The University Admission Support System solves this by aggregating all admission circulars into one intelligent, personalized interface.

What we cover:

  • Personalized circular feed matched to your GPA, group, and preferences
  • Automated email alerts the moment matching circulars are published
  • Application tracker with a full journey timeline per university
  • Google Calendar auto-sync for exam dates
  • AI counselor that gives you a real strategy — not generic advice
  • Seat availability visualization and university comparison
